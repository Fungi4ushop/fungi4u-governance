## Work Intake and Selection Model

Status:
Identified

Observation:

The operating system provides strong governance for approved work through:

Review
↓
Formalisation
↓
Project
↓
Publication
↓
Closure

However, governance before approval remains weakly defined.

FUTURE_CANDIDATES appears to occupy a lifecycle position upstream of OPEN_ACTIONS.

OPEN_ACTIONS contains approved work.

FUTURE_CANDIDATES contains candidate work.

Potential Scope:

* Candidate identification
* Candidate evaluation
* Operating Review
* Work selection
* Candidate prioritisation
* Promotion rules from FUTURE_CANDIDATES to active governance work
* Relationship between FUTURE_CANDIDATES and OPEN_ACTIONS

Reason:

The pilot revealed that the operating system is well-defined after work selection but less defined before work selection.

Future Design Question:

How does candidate work enter the operating system and become approved work?
